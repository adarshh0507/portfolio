WYNKO Deep Dive

Focus • Study • Together

WYNKO was created out of a personal frustration experienced by modern students: endless digital distraction, isolated study sessions, and lack of real accountability. WYNKO bridges gamified focus mechanisms with a social accountability layer.

Key Platform Capabilities

1. Focus Sessions: Customizable Pomodoro intervals and flow states with ambient binaural audio generators.
2. App Blocking: Intelligent friction blocks distracting social media apps and browser tabs during active study clocks.
3. Group Study: Virtual synchronized silent focus rooms with cameras/screen presence or avatar study squads.
4. Study Challenges: Daily quests, subject marathons, and collegiate leaderboard sprints.
5. Study Communities: Niche topic channels (Data Science, Math, Engineering) for peer review and shared study decks.
6. Rewards / WYNKOINS: Students earn in-app WYNKOINS for verified focus hours, redeemable for study perks and partner vouchers.

Adarsh Singh's Role: CMO

Leading the go-to-market playbook, managing social presence, establishing student campus ambassadorships, and crafting high-converting visual branding for prospective students.
